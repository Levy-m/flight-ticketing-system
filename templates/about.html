About SkyLink Airlines

Proudly Kenyan. Connecting people, places and possibilities.

Our story

SkyLink Airlines was founded in Nairobi in 2012 with two turboprop aircraft and a single route to Mombasa. Today we operate a modern fleet of Bombardier Q400, Embraer E190 and Boeing 787 aircraft, flying to destinations across Kenya, East Africa and the Middle East.

Over the years we have carried more than three million passengers, built a reputation for punctuality and friendly service, and invested in digital tools — like this online booking platform — so customers no longer have to queue at our offices to buy a ticket.

Our mission

To provide safe, reliable and affordable air travel that connects communities and businesses across Africa and beyond, with service that makes every passenger feel welcome.

Our vision

To be East Africa's most loved airline — recognised for punctuality, innovation and care for our passengers and the environment.
